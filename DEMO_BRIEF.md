# AB Building Services Nottingham Ltd - Local Concept Demo

## Scope

One static, local-only UK electrician service page. Core visitor task: understand the service, see credible proof, and start an enquiry by phone or email. No form submission, data collection, booking, payment, login, deployment, or outreach.

## Verified customer facts used

- Mansfield-based AB Building Services Nottingham Ltd is represented on Checkatrade and MyJobQuote as an electrical-services business.
- Public evidence supports electrical installation, consumer units, EICR, fault finding, EV charging and related domestic work.
- Checkatrade lists Mr Alex Bregu as Owner and recent customer reviews.
- Public phone: 07375620343. Public email: [REDACTED_EMAIL].

## Customer-visible content boundaries

Shown: a focused electrical-services presentation, three core service groups, public contact details, and four recorded business-attributable Checkatrade profile images.

Not shown: unverified prices, guarantees, project claims, accreditations, service-area promises, invented testimonials, photos claimed as customer work, booking, or a contact form.

## Design read

Brand landing page for local homeowners who need a trustworthy electrician, with a precise, reassuring industrial-residential language. Soul: Controlled Utility. Dials: SOUL 7, SPECTACLE 3, DENSITY 5; design variance 7, motion intensity 3, visual density 5.

## Skill impact

- `finesse-ui`: graphite substrate, restrained copper accent, grain, asymmetrical split hero, low-motion visual hierarchy.
- `design-taste-frontend`: rejects generic three-card row, AI-purple glow, fake metrics, decorative labels, and the existing site's mismatched building-maintenance language.
- Direction revision: original concept visuals were removed from customer display after the customer-specific authenticity review. The page now uses four visually checked, business-attributable Checkatrade profile images; their URLs, hashes, placements and B-class limitations are recorded in the internal ledger.
- `media-use`: reviewed for asset handling. Its first-run path requires installation/sign-in, which is outside this authorization; no provider or external media workflow was invoked.

## Acceptance target

Desktop 1440x900 and Mobile 390x844: readable, no horizontal overflow, every asset loads, primary contact links work, reduced motion preserves the page, and no internal governance text is visible.
